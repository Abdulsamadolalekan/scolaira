# Product Discovery Backlog

> Separate VALIDATED / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN.
> UNKNOWN requirements must NOT silently become architecture.

---

## How to Read This Backlog

- **VALIDATED** — we have direct evidence (founder directive, legal requirement, observed school practice, or verified technical reality).
- **ASSUMED** — we believe this is true based on reasonable prior knowledge; will be validated with pilot schools; current architecture assumes it but we have a fallback plan if it's wrong.
- **UNKNOWN** — we don't yet know the answer. The architecture is designed to NOT depend on any specific answer (e.g., by making the relevant component pluggable or by deferring the feature). Each UNKNOWN has a discovery step planned.

## UNKNOWNs That Are Explicitly Deferred (Do Not Block Architecture)

These are items that would be P3 features even if known; no need to resolve them for pilot:

- Multi-currency (USD/GBP for international school branches).
- USSD payment flows.
- Standing-order / direct-debit arrangements.
- Native mobile apps.
- Multi-bank virtual accounts.
- Parent self-service historical statement access beyond signed links.
- AI anything (intentionally out of scope).

## Process

- Items move from U → A/V as discovery proceeds.
- A items that are invalidated become U with new discovery planned, or move to decision log if change is strategic.
- Decisions that change architecture or product direction MUST go through `/docs/DECISIONS.md` with benefit/risk/migration cost described.
- Items never silently move from U/A to V without evidence.
